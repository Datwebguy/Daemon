## Overview
2-week hackathon to build a new experience powered by Chog NFTs.  
Prize pool: $15K (can be reduced to $10K if quality threshold is not met). Top 50 projects win.  
14 days to build, then 7 days of judging. Any category. Solo or team. Vibecoding allowed.

**Hard requirement**: The Chog NFT must be essential. If you remove the NFT, the experience must stop working or change meaningfully. This is a pass/fail gate.

## Registration Answers (Draft)

**Project Pitch (one line)**  
Daemon turns passive Chog NFTs into automated smart accounts that earn yield through pluggable strategies, get compounded by anyone for a bounty, and carry a living personality that friends can hype — all on Monad, with no operator.

**Why the Chog NFT matters**  
Daemon binds the smart account, its strategies, mood, boost state, and performance history to the Chog token ID via ERC-6551. The NFT is the master ownership title. Wherever the NFT goes, the account, assets, and personality follow. Take the NFT out: the account has no owner, nobody can deposit, withdraw, sweep or choose strategies, and the Daemon stops compounding and hyping. Users keep full custody with a 1-click sweep at all times.
